Depth¶

Source-contaminated regions were masked, and 10,000 random circular apertures with radii matched to the PSF FWHM were placed in each of 20 iterations. The 5σ limiting flux was derived from five times the standard deviation of the aperture fluxes, averaged across all iterations, and converted to an AB magnitude.
